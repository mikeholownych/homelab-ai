# Q-BUDGET: production reasoning budget, from bounded evidence (2026-10-08)

Question: which server-side `--reasoning-budget` should each pool serve? Operator ruling: quality over speed; bounded
reasoning is mandatory and the value comes from this sweep.

## Method

- Path: remote client (controller) → TLS gateway `https://10.0.8.5:8443` → candidate alias. No direct worker request;
  nothing initiated on the inference host. Qualification-scoped token piped from the host's root-only file.
- Per budget, `evaluations/engx/qbudget.sh` redeploys the candidate through `playbooks/candidate.yml`
  (`-e candidate_reasoning_budget=N`), refuses to measure unless `/v1/models` reports `"budget": N`, then runs the hard
  corpus (10 tasks, H01-H10), 3 reps, max 3 attempts, `max_tokens = N + 8192`, temperature 0.
- Reasoning/answer tokens are exact counts with the worker's own tokenizer via the gateway `/v1/tokenize`.
- Raw evidence: `results/qbudget-20261008/` (per-budget result JSON, run log, deploy log); stage log
  `results/qbudget-stageA-20261008T0642.out`.

## Flash-Next (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-GSQ-RCO-IQ1_M, artifact sha256:935587187e4b…)

| Budget | Validated | Per rep | First attempt | Median s to validated | Mean s/task | Mean tokens/task | Flaky | Always fail |
|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 2048 | 80.0% | 9/7/8 | 60.0% | 162.2 | 272.3 | 7,547 | H03 1/3, H06 2/3 | H07 |
| **4096** | **90.0%** | 9/9/9 | 73.3% | 271.5 | 386.8 | 9,176 | none | H07 |
| 8192 | 90.0% | 9/9/9 | 73.3% | 495.1 | 665.6 | 14,149 | none | H07 |

Serving behaviour (all attempts):

| Budget | Attempts | Budget reached | Median reasoning tokens | Termination | Median decode tok/s |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 2048 | 49 | 100% | 2,065 | stop ×49 | 19.4 |
| 4096 | 43 | 95% | 4,113 | stop ×43 | 19.2 |
| 8192 | 42 | 95% | 8,209 | stop ×42 | 18.9 |

Findings:
1. The bound works: every attempt terminated with `stop` and a final answer; no output-limit truncation and no
   unbounded reasoning (the original defect) at any budget.
2. The model spends whatever it is given (95-100% of attempts reach the budget), so the budget sets cost directly.
3. 2048 costs quality (80%, two tasks become flaky). 8192 buys nothing over 4096 (identical 90%, identical per-task
   pattern) at 1.7x the time and 1.5x the tokens. 12288 is therefore not run: doubling from 4096 already showed no gain.
4. H07 fails at every budget, as it did in Phase B direct; it is a capability limit, not a budget effect.
5. Through the gateway, 4096 reproduces the Phase B direct-to-worker result (90%), so the gateway path costs no quality.

**Decision: Flash-Next serves with `--reasoning-budget 4096`** (already the `flashnext-deep` profile default and the
interim containment value on the deep pool; now evidence-backed).
